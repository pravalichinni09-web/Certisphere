# CertiSphere — Promotional Video Script
**Format:** 90-Second Brand Promo | Voiceover + Screen Demo Cuts
**Tone:** Premium, confident, globally credible

---

## ◼ SCENE 1 — THE PROBLEM (0:00–0:15)
**Visual:** Montage — cluttered spreadsheets, email chains, sticky notes, missed deadlines, stressed auditors on calls. Black & white filter. Slow cut rhythm.

**VO (calm, measured):**
> "Certification bodies run on one of the most complex operational models in business — multi-standard, multi-country, multi-team. And most of them are still managing it... in spreadsheets."

**On-screen text:** `250+ steps. 6 roles. Countless email threads.`

---

## ◼ SCENE 2 — THE REVEAL (0:15–0:25)
**Visual:** Dark screen. A glowing sphere animation expands. CertiSphere logo fades in with the tagline. Colour floods in — brand blue and gold.

**VO:**
> "Introducing **CertiSphere** — the global certification management platform built for the way auditing actually works."

**On-screen text:** `"Compliance builds trust. Certification proves it."`

---

## ◼ SCENE 3 — THE PLATFORM (0:25–0:50)
**Visual:** Smooth product walkthrough — dashboard, workflow tracker, scheduling screen, certificate issuance. Each screen gets 2–3 seconds. Clean UI animation.

**VO:**
> "One unified platform. From the first client enquiry to final certificate delivery — every stage tracked, every role accountable, every deadline visible."

**VO continues with screen cuts:**

> **"Client Enquiry → Application Dispatch."** *(cut to enquiry form being filled)*
> "Capture leads. Send application packs by email, WhatsApp, or portal link — all tracked."

> **"Audit Scheduling."** *(cut to schedule calendar with team assignment)*
> "Propose dates, assign auditor teams, route through client and auditor confirmations — automatically."

> **"Stage 1 & Stage 2 Audit."** *(cut to audit workflow 14-stage tracker)*
> "A single 14-stage workflow tracker every certification flows through. No step ever falls through the cracks."

> **"Findings & Corrective Actions."** *(cut to non-conformity cards)*
> "Log non-conformities, release them to the auditee, and track closure — with full root-cause accountability."

> **"Certificate Delivery."** *(cut to certificate being issued)*
> "Issue, seal, and deliver certificates — with a complete, tamper-proof audit trail."

---

## ◼ SCENE 4 — AI INTELLIGENCE (0:50–1:05)
**Visual:** QCSAI assistant panel, Smart Scheduler populating a calendar, Anomaly Detection flagging a risk, Evidence Validation scoring a document. Fast-cut, slightly futuristic feel.

**VO:**
> "CertiSphere is powered by embedded AI — not bolted on. Not a chatbot. **Wired into every step.**"

**VO continues:**
> "Smart Scheduler assigns the right auditor to the right client, weighing expertise, workload, geography and history in seconds."
> "Anomaly Detection surfaces risks before they become non-conformities."
> "Auto Report Generation delivers formal audit reports from a single click."
> "AI-assisted workflows cut time-to-compliance by up to **60%.**"

**On-screen text:** `Smart Checklist · Anomaly Detection · Evidence Validation · Auto Report · Auditor Assignment AI`

---

## ◼ SCENE 5 — ROLE-BASED & GLOBAL (1:05–1:15)
**Visual:** World map with connection lines lighting up — country flags appearing. Split-screen showing 5 different role dashboards simultaneously.

**VO:**
> "Six purpose-built role portals. One platform — for Management, Coordinators, Lead Auditors, Auditors, Clients and Client Coordinators. Operating seamlessly across every country you certify in."

**On-screen stats (animated counters):**
`250+ Certifications Delivered · 200+ Happy Clients · 98% Success Rate · 20+ Expert Auditors`

---

## ◼ SCENE 6 — STANDARDS SUPPORTED (1:15–1:22)
**Visual:** Clean icon grid — ISO badges appearing one by one with a soft glow.

**VO:**
> "ISO 9001. ISO 14001. ISO 45001. ISO 27001. ISO 22000. ISO 13485. SOC 2. HIPAA. GMP. And more."

**On-screen text:** `ISO/IEC 17021-1 Compliant`

---

## ◼ SCENE 7 — CLOSE (1:22–1:30)
**Visual:** CertiSphere logo on dark background. Website URL. Subtle particle animation (certification seals drifting).

**VO:**
> "**CertiSphere.** Structured certification operations — delivered globally."

**On-screen text (sequential fade):**
```
www.qcertisphere.com
Powered by QMICS Solutions
```

**Final frame:** Logo lockup with tagline `"Enquiry to renewal. Across countries and standards."`
