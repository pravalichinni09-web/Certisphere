## 2. The Problem CertiSphere Solves

Certification bodies (CBs) face a recurring set of pains:

- **Fragmented tooling** — enquiries in email, applications in PDFs, schedules in spreadsheets, certificates in Word. Nothing is connected.
- **Manual handoffs** — a coordinator schedules an audit, then has to email the auditor, the lead auditor and the client separately. Status gets lost.
- **No single source of truth** — Management can't see programme health; auditees don't know where their audit stands.
- **Impartiality & audit-trail risk** — hard to prove independence, rotation and a defensible audit trail for accreditation.
- **Multi-standard, multi-country complexity** — ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 27001 and more, across time zones and currencies.
- **Surveillance sprawl** — annual surveillance and 3-yearly recertification are easy to miss when tracked by hand.

**CertiSphere fixes this by making one platform own the entire certification lifecycle.**

## 3. How It Works — The 14-Stage Certification Pipeline

Every certification flows through one tracked pipeline, from first contact to ongoing surveillance. Each stage is owned by a specific role and hands off automatically to the next.

1. **Enquiry** — A prospect submits a public enquiry; it lands in the Coordinator's inbox.
2. **Qualification** — Coordinator reviews and converts the enquiry into an application.
3. **Application Dispatch** — The digital application form is sent by email, WhatsApp or portal link, with delivery tracking.
4. **Application Received** — The client (auditee) fills and submits; status flips to Received.
5. **Review & Approval** — Coordinator reviews, approves (or holds/rejects with notes).
6. **Client Registration** — An auditee record is created with an auto-generated client code.
7. **Contract & Agreement** — Registration and contract are issued; the client confirms.
8. **Auditor Onboarding** — Auditor competence is validated end-to-end: Coordinator → Lead Auditor → Management.
9. **Audit Scheduling** — Dates proposed, teams assigned, routed through auditee + auditor confirmations.
10. **Audit Confirmation** — Auditor and auditee confirm the schedule.
11. **Conduct Audit** — The audit is carried out against the standard and a clause-mapped checklist.
12. **Findings & Actions** — Non-conformities are logged, released to the auditee, and corrective actions accepted.
13. **Certification** — The certificate is issued, sealed and delivered with a full audit trail.
14. **Surveillance & Recertification** — Annual surveillance and 3-yearly recertification are scheduled automatically.

**The key idea:** an action in one role (approve a schedule, log a finding, issue a certificate) instantly surfaces in the next role's view — no re-keying, no lost handoffs.

## 4. Built for Six Roles — Each Sees Only What They Own

CertiSphere is role-based. Every stakeholder gets a focused workspace:

- **Management** — Dashboard, Audit Assignments, Auditor Approvals, Client Onboarding, Client Credentials, Global Settings. The command centre for programme health and governance.
- **Audit Coordinator** — Enquiries, Applications, Client Registration, Scheduling, Checklists, Confirmations, Certifications, Reports, Certificate Delivery. The operational hub.
- **Lead Auditor** — Auditor Approvals, Schedules, Conduct Audit, Reports, Action Review & Approval. Owns audit quality and sign-off.
- **Auditor** — Schedule Confirmations, Conduct Audit, Reports, Action Review. The field executor.
- **Auditee (Client)** — Workflow view, Pending Applications, Schedule Approvals, Audit Actions, Submit Report. Full transparency into their own certification journey.
- **Client Coordinator** — Organization View, Certifications, Due-Date Tracker, Schedules, Reports, Notifications. The client-side account manager.

## 6. The AI Layer — CertiSphere's Standout Advantage

CertiSphere embeds AI directly into the certification workflow (works with a heuristic engine offline, and upgrades to full AI when connected). Highlights by role:

**Audit Coordinator**
- **Audit Intelligence** — AI audit planner, non-conformance predictor, smart checklist generator, finding classifier.

## 9. Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React 18 + TypeScript, built with Vite — a fast, modern single-page application.
- **Routing & UX:** React Router; component-driven UI with a light, brand-consistent design system; lucide icon set; Chart.js for analytics.
- **Backend & data:** Supabase (Postgres + Auth) for cloud sync, with a resilient local-first storage layer so the app stays usable offline / in demo mode.
- **AI:** Claude (Anthropic) integration for document analysis, planning, anomaly detection and the QCSAI assistant — with deterministic heuristic engines as an always-on fallback.
- **Documents:** in-app generation of applications, schedules and certificates (Word/PDF), inline preview, and QR-coded certificates.
- **Integrations:** email (EmailJS), WhatsApp and portal-link dispatch.
- **Architecture principles:** role-based access, event-driven live refresh across roles, and a defensive data layer hardened against malformed data.

## 13. Business Strategy

> *The following are strategic recommendations to shape as you validate with the market — not fixed claims.*

**Target market (ICP)**
- Primary: Small-to-mid-size **certification bodies (CBs)** running ISO management-system certifications (9001/14001/45001/27001), especially multi-standard/multi-country operators.
- Secondary: **Inspection & testing bodies**, training + certification providers, and in-house compliance teams that manage recurring audits.
- Beachhead: CBs in India / the Gulf / SE Asia (aligned with QMICS's footprint) before broader expansion.

**Positioning**
- "The **operating system for certification bodies** — the only platform that owns the whole lifecycle *and* is AI-native." Position against point tools (scheduling-only, or generic audit apps) and against spreadsheets/manual process.

**Value proposition (quantify in pilots)**
- More audits per auditor (less admin), faster time-to-certificate, fewer missed surveillance dates, and audit-ready traceability for accreditation.

**Go-to-market**
1. **Land with a free, seeded demo** — the app already boots with realistic data, so prospects can self-evaluate instantly. Offer guided demos (use Script C).
2. **Design-partner programme** — onboard 3–5 CBs at a discount in exchange for feedback + case studies.
3. **Channel via accreditation & standards ecosystems** — partnerships, associations, LinkedIn thought leadership, and QMICS's existing client base.
4. **Content engine** — ISO/17021 how-to guides, "certification ops" playbooks, and the video assets above.

**Revenue / pricing model (SaaS)**
- **Per-seat by role tier** (Management/Coordinator seats priced higher than Auditor/Auditee viewer seats), or
- **Per active certification / per audit** usage pricing that scales with the CB's volume, or
- **Platform tiers:** Starter (single standard, limited seats) → Growth (multi-standard, AI features) → Enterprise (multi-country, SSO, custom SLAs, on-prem/region hosting).
- **AI as an upsell** — heuristic features included; full Claude-powered AI on higher tiers or metered.
- Add-ons: implementation/onboarding, data migration, custom certificate branding, API/integrations.

**Competitive advantage / moat**
- End-to-end lifecycle coverage + AI depth + ISO/IEC 17021-1 alignment is hard to replicate piecemeal.
- Role-based, connected workflow creates switching cost once a CB runs live programmes on it.
- Data compounding: the more audits run, the sharper the AI planning/anomaly models get.

**Roadmap levers (expansion revenue)**
- Accreditation-body reporting exports, deeper analytics, mobile auditor app, e-signatures, client self-service portal upgrades, marketplace of standard-specific checklist packs, and API for ecosystem integrations.

**Key metrics to track**
- Time-to-certificate, audits/auditor/month, surveillance on-time rate, activation (demo → live), net revenue retention, and AI-feature adoption.
